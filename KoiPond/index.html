Summer Koi Pond

夏の錦鯉池 · Ink & Watercolor

A quiet overhead Japanese garden pond rendered with an ink-and-watercolor sensibility. Move your hands above the water to brush its surface, and pinch your fingers to feed the koi.

🌊 Touch & brush water
🤏 Pinch to feed
🍃 Or click/drag mouse
Hand tracking runs 100% client-side in your browser. Video is never transmitted or saved.

Pond Interaction

·
Water surface deformation: Move your fingertips across the pond to displace the watercolor surface, leaving tapered fluid wakes and organic broken ripples.
·
Pinch to feed: Pinch your thumb and index fingertip together to drop small food pellets. Slender watercolor koi will swim over to eat.
·
Subtle reflection: Your hands appear as an elusive silhouette reflection on the water. Toggle it with the top button.
·
Camera selection: Switch between your MacBook built-in camera, external cameras, or iPhone through Continuity Camera in the top-bar dropdown.
·
Fullscreen & mouse: Tap Fullscreen for an immersive view. If webcam is unavailable or denied, click and drag anywhere to brush the water and feed.
